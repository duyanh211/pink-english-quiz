Pink English Word Chain dictionary notes

The bundled words.json is a 20,000-entry offline game lexicon. The selected headwords are derived from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (CMUdict) and filtered/ranked with a frequency list.
CMUdict copyright: Copyright (C) 1993-2015 Carnegie Mellon University. All rights reserved. CMU's distribution terms permit use and redistribution with acknowledgment; retain the corresponding license notice when redistributing derived material.

CMUdict project: https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict
The dictionary is not exhaustive. If a valid English word is missing, the app reports 'not in the built-in list' rather than claiming the word is not English.
The CEFR level does not restrict a player's accepted word. All users use the same global dictionary.
